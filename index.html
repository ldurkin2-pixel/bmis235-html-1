K2: The Savage Mountain

A page about the most notorious mountain in mountaineering

K2 is the second tallest mountain in the world, standing at 28,251 feet above sea level. It borders Pakistan and China in the Karakoram Range.

K2 was nicknamed the Savage Mountain by George Bell, who called it "a savage mountain that tries to kill you."

Facts about K2:

  • K2's mortality rate is 22-25%, making it one of the most dangerous mountains above 8,000 meters to climb.
  • The first ascent took place on July 31, 1954. The first ever winter ascent was achieved on January 16, 2021, by a team of 10 Nepali mountaineers led by Nimsdai Purja, who has a Netflix documentary that encapsulated him summiting all 14 8,000+ meter peaks in 7 months.
  • K2 has an iconic ice gully located 8,200 meters (~27,000 feet) called the Bottleneck that sits below a massive serac (hanging ice cliff).The serac often collapses, sweeping away climbers bellow.
